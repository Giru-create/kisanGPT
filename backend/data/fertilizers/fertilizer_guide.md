# Fertilizer Guide for Indian Crops

## Nitrogen (N) Fertilizers

### Urea (46% N)

- Most commonly used nitrogen fertilizer
- Apply in 2-3 splits for better utilization
- Best time: Just before irrigation
- Recommended: 120-150 kg/ha for rice, 100-120 kg/ha for wheat

### Ammonium Sulphate (21% N)

- Also provides sulfur
- Good for oilseeds and pulses
- Apply as basal dose

## Phosphorus (P) Fertilizers

### DAP (Di-Ammonium Phosphate - 18% N, 46% P2O5)

- Most popular phosphatic fertilizer
- Apply at sowing time as basal dose
- Recommended: 60-80 kg/ha for most crops

### SSP (Single Super Phosphate - 16% P2O5)

- Also provides calcium and sulfur
- Good for pulses and oilseeds
- Apply as basal dose

## Potassium (K) Fertilizers

### Muriate of Potash (MOP - 60% K2O)

- Most common potassium source
- Apply as basal dose
- Recommended: 40-60 kg/ha for rice, 30-40 kg/ha for wheat

## Micronutrients

### Zinc Sulphate

- Deficiency common in rice and wheat
- Apply 25 kg/ha as soil application
- Foliar spray: 0.5% solution

### Boron

- Important for cotton, groundnut, and vegetables
- Apply 10-15 kg/ha of Borax
- Foliar spray: 0.2% solution

## Organic Manures

### Vermicompost

- Rich in nutrients and beneficial microorganisms
- Apply 2-5 tonnes/ha
- Improves soil structure and water retention

### Farm Yard Manure (FYM)

- Traditional organic manure
- Apply 5-10 tonnes/ha before sowing
- Improves soil fertility over time

## Integrated Nutrient Management

- Combine organic and inorganic fertilizers
- Use soil testing to determine exact needs
- Follow 4R approach: Right source, Right rate, Right time, Right place
