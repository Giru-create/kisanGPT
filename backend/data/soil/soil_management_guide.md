# Soil Management Guide

## Soil Types in India

### Alluvial Soil

- Found in: Indo-Gangetic plains, Brahmaputra valley
- Crops: Rice, wheat, sugarcane, cotton
- Fertilizer need: Medium to high

### Black Soil (Regur)

- Found in: Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh
- Crops: Cotton, soybean, groundnut, jowar
- Fertilizer need: Low to medium

### Red Soil

- Found in: Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh
- Crops: Rice, groundnut, tobacco, cotton
- Fertilizer need: Medium to high

### Laterite Soil

- Found in: Kerala, Karnataka, Odisha
- Crops: Tea, coffee, rubber, cashew
- Fertilizer need: High (nutrient leaching common)

## Soil Testing

### Why Test Soil?

- Determine nutrient deficiencies
- Avoid over/under fertilization
- Save money on fertilizers
- Improve crop yields

### How to Collect Samples

1. Collect from 15-20 spots in your field
2. Take samples from 0-15 cm depth
3. Mix all samples thoroughly
4. Take 500g as representative sample
5. Air dry and送 to nearest soil testing lab

## Soil Health Card

- Government scheme for soil testing
- Provides customized fertilizer recommendations
- Available at local agriculture offices
- Free of cost for farmers

## Improving Soil Health

### Green Manuring

- Grow dhaincha, sunnhemp, or legumes
- Plough back at flowering stage
- Adds nitrogen and organic matter

### Crop Rotation

- Rotate cereals with pulses
- Follow rice with wheat or oilseeds
- Break pest and disease cycles

### Mulching

- Cover soil with crop residues
- Reduces moisture evaporation
- Controls weed growth
- Adds organic matter as it decomposes

### Reduced Tillage

- Minimize soil disturbance
- Preserves soil structure
- Reduces erosion
- Saves fuel and labor costs
