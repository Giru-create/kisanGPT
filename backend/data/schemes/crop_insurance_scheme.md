# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

## Overview

PMFBY is a crop insurance scheme launched in 2016 to protect farmers against crop loss due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases.

## Benefits

- Comprehensive risk coverage
- Low premium rates for farmers
- Quick claim settlement
- Technology-driven (satellite, drones)

## Premium Rates

- Kharif: 2% of sum insured
- Rabi: 1.5% of sum insured
- Commercial/Horticultural: 5% of sum insured

## Coverage

- Natural calamities: Drought, flood, hailstorm, pest attack
- Post-harvest losses (up to 14 days)
- Localized calamities: Inundation, landslides, hailstorm

## Eligibility

- All farmers (sharecroppers and tenant farmers included)
- Compulsory for loanee farmers
- Optional for non-loanee farmers

## How to Apply

1. Visit nearest bank or CSC centre
2. Fill application form with land details
3. Submit Aadhaar and bank details
4. Pay premium amount

## Claim Process

- Government notifies crop loss areas
- Automatic claim processing using technology
- Compensation transferred to bank account
- No paperwork required for farmers
