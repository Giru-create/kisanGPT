# Common Crop Diseases in India

## Rice Diseases

### Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae)

- Symptoms: Diamond-shaped lesions on leaves, neck rot
- Treatment: Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L
- Prevention: Use resistant varieties, balanced nitrogen

### Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae)

- Symptoms: Yellow stripes on leaf margins, wilting
- Treatment: Copper hydroxide spray
- Prevention: Use healthy seeds, avoid excess nitrogen

## Wheat Diseases

### Rust (Puccinia spp.)

- Symptoms: Orange-brown pustules on leaves
- Treatment: Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 ml/L
- Prevention: Use rust-resistant varieties

### Powdery Mildew

- Symptoms: White powdery growth on leaves
- Treatment: Sulfur 80% WP @ 2.5 g/L
- Prevention: Ensure proper spacing, avoid overhead irrigation

## Cotton Diseases

### Bacterial Blight

- Symptoms: Water-soaked spots on leaves, angular leaf spots
- Treatment: Copper oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L
- Prevention: Use certified seeds, crop rotation

## Vegetable Diseases

### Early Blight (Tomato, Potato)

- Symptoms: Concentric rings on leaves, dark spots
- Treatment: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L
- Prevention: Crop rotation, remove infected debris

### Downy Mildew (Cucurbits)

- Symptoms: Yellow patches on upper leaf surface, white fuzz below
- Treatment: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb @ 2.5 g/L
- Prevention: Ensure good air circulation, avoid overhead watering
