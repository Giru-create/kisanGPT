# Weather Guide for Indian Farmers

## Monsoon Preparedness

### Before Monsoon (May-June)

- Clean drainage channels in fields
- Repair bunds and field boundaries
- Store seeds and fertilizers in dry places
- Prepare nurseries for kharif crops

### During Monsoon (July-September)

- Monitor weather forecasts daily
- Ensure proper field drainage
- Avoid irrigation during heavy rainfall
- Watch for pest and disease outbreaks

## Temperature Management

### Heat Wave (April-June)

- Irrigate fields early morning or late evening
- Provide shade for livestock
- Use mulching to retain soil moisture
- Avoid spraying chemicals during peak heat

### Cold Wave (December-January)

- Cover sensitive crops with plastic sheets or straw
- Irrigate before cold wave to protect roots
- Avoid irrigation at night
- Use frost protection methods for vegetables

## Wind Protection

- Install wind breaks (hedgerows, fencing)
- Secure greenhouse structures before storms
- Avoid pesticide spraying when wind speed exceeds 15 km/h
- Stake young plants in windy areas

## Rainfall Tracking

- Maintain a rain gauge in your farm
- Record daily rainfall during monsoon
- Adjust irrigation based on rainfall received
- Calculate water balance for your crops
