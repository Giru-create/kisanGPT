# PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

## Overview

PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme launched on 24th February 2019 to provide income support to all landholding farmers' families.

## Benefits

- Direct income support of Rs. 6,000 per year
- Paid in three equal instalments of Rs. 2,000 each
- Transferred directly to farmers' bank accounts

## Eligibility

- All landholding farmer families
- Subject to certain exclusions like institutional landholders, former/current constitutional post holders, etc.

## How to Apply

1. Visit the PM-KISAN portal at pmkisan.gov.in
2. Click on 'New Farmer Registration'
3. Enter Aadhaar number and other required details
4. Submit the application

## Documents Required

- Aadhaar card
- Bank account details
- Land ownership documents
