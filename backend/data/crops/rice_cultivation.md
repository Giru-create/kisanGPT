# Rice Cultivation Guide

## Varieties

### HYV (High Yielding Varieties)

- Pusa Basmati 1121 (Basmati)
- IR-64 (Non-basmati)
- Swarna (Popular in eastern India)
- Sona Masuri (Andhra Pradesh)

### Duration

- Early season: 90-100 days
- Medium season: 110-120 days
- Late season: 130-150 days

## Land Preparation

1. Plough field 2-3 times after first rain
2. Level the field properly
3. Maintain 2-3 cm standing water
4. Apply basal fertilizers before transplanting

## Transplanting

- Seedling age: 20-25 days
- Spacing: 15 x 10 cm
- 2-3 seedlings per hill
- Transplant at 2 cm water depth

## Water Management

- Maintain 5 cm water during vegetative stage
- Drain 1-2 days before applying fertilizers
- Intermittent drainage at flowering
- Drain 15 days before harvest

## Nutrient Management

- N: 120 kg/ha in 3 splits
- P: 60 kg/ha as basal
- K: 40 kg/ha in 2 splits
- Zinc: 25 kg/ha ZnSO4 if deficient

## Harvesting

- Harvest when 80% grains turn golden
- Moisture content should be 20-25%
- Thresh within 2 days of cutting
- Dry to 14% moisture for storage
